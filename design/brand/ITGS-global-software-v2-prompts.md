## Initial prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one exceptionally refined original corporate logo for ITGS, a global enterprise software and infrastructure company. The user wants the quiet confidence, clarity and recognition of a major worldwide software company, without imitating any existing company logo.
Art direction: elegant restrained Swiss modernism, beautifully balanced custom geometric sans-serif capital letters spelling exactly "ITGS". Medium-bold weight, spacious open counters, precise optical kerning, common cap height, calm sophisticated proportions. Develop a subtle distinctive structural connection in the I and T, inspired by vertical architectural infrastructure, while preserving immediate individual letter recognition. G and S should be graceful and open with carefully softened geometric curves. This is a serious timeless software identity, never chunky industrial stencil type or gaming type.
Make the typography the hero. The recognizable signature is a refined IT architectural joint that can also stand alone as the compact monogram. Keep it simple enough to draw from memory and reproduce at small sizes. Avoid a generic symbol appended to a normal font.
Presentation: immaculate flat logo on pure white with very generous whitespace. One large primary ITGS wordmark centered in the upper two-thirds, in deep ink navy #102640 with one discreet cloud-blue #1677E8 structural accent. Along the bottom, three small understated supporting specimens with ample separation: the exact wordmark in pure black; the matching IT monogram in blue; the exact wordmark in white on a small solid navy rectangular swatch. These are supporting applications of ONE consistent design, not different concepts.
Only visible text is ITGS in the marks. No headings, labels, taglines, watermarks, mockups, gradients, texture, shadows, 3D, outlines, roof silhouettes, clouds, shields, globes, wifi, circuitry, four-square windows, swooshes, or decorative network lines. Razor-sharp flat vector-style edges, deliberate crafted geometry. Landscape presentation.

## Final refinement prompt

Correct the presentation of this ITGS logo. Preserve the exact letter shapes and distinctive blue angled cap above the I. REPLACE THE ENTIRE BLACK BACKGROUND WITH PURE OPAQUE WHITE #FFFFFF. Remove absolutely all blue glow, lighting, shadow, gradients, and transparency. Main wordmark must be completely solid dark navy #102640 with a flat blue cap #1677E8, extremely legible against white. Keep the three supporting samples along the bottom: solid black wordmark on white at left, flat blue IT monogram on white at center, white wordmark inside a solid navy rectangle at right. The rest of the entire canvas is WHITE. Crisp flat graphic design, no effects. Same spacious composition and exact typography. This is a logo artwork sheet, not a cinematic scene.
