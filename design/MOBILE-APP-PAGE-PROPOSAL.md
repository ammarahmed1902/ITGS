# Mobile App Development service page proposal

The Mobile App Development page follows the Web Development page's visual structure while using its own appointment-booking concept. The hero shows two legible phone screens: service and time selection, plus a confirmation with booking details. In the larger demonstration, visitors can switch the second screen between Details, Confirmed, and Manage states.

The example is marked **ITGS internal concept**, **Self-initiated concept · Not client work**, and **Illustrative data**. It represents no client or real booking. The page has three use cases, three explanatory annotations, four deliverables, four process stages with named outputs, four FAQs, a navy CTA, and the shared compact footer.

Publication status: **proposed design**. Confirm platform recommendations, integration feasibility, release responsibilities, pricing, timelines, and maintenance terms before treating the copy as an operational offer. App-store approval is controlled by the stores and is not guaranteed.
